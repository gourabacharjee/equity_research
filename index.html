Module 01 · Beginner

1. Fundamentals of Equity Research

Equity Research is the forensic analysis of public companies to assess their intrinsic value and provide actionable recommendations (BUY, HOLD, SELL) to institutional and retail investors.

💡 The Analyst's Core Mission An equity research analyst is a corporate detective. Your job is not to passively repeat what management says in their press releases, but to discover what the market is missing or mispricing.

Sell-Side vs. Buy-Side Analysis

The financial research industry is structured into two fundamental divisions:

Module 05 · Report Architecture

5. Institutional Equity Research Report Blueprint

A high-impact equity research report follows an 8-section institutional structure designed to deliver immediate conviction.

1. Executive Summary & Thesis

State your recommendation (BUY), target price, expected 12M return, and 3 differentiated structural catalysts immediately on page 1.

2. Company & Moat Overview

Breakdown revenue by segment, geography, customer retention, and evaluate Porter's Five Forces and pricing power.

3. Financial Forecasts (3-Year Model)

Provide audited historicals (FY23-FY25) alongside projected line-by-line Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Free Cash Flow forecasts.

4. Valuation & Sensitivity Matrix

Combine DCF (WACC sensitivity table) and Peer Trading Multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA) to triangulate a credible price target.

Module 06 · AI Modernization

6. Leveraging Artificial Intelligence in Equity Research

Top institutional analysts use AI as a high-velocity copilot for transcript scrubbing, footnote analysis, and preliminary memo drafting while applying human oversight.

⚠️ The Golden Rule: Human-In-The-Loop Verification Always provide source filings (10-K, Annual Reports) to the LLM and verify every extracted number. Never allow an AI model to guess valuation parameters without primary documentation.

Copyable Institutional AI Prompts

Prompt 1: Earnings Call Forensic Red Flag Analysis
Act as an institutional sell-side equity research analyst. I have uploaded the Q4 earnings call transcript for [Company Name]. Please provide: 1. Executive Tone Analysis: Is management defensive, confident, or conservative regarding forward revenue guidance? 2. Margin Headwinds: What specific cost pressures (wage inflation, raw materials, capex) were acknowledged? 3. Analyst Pushback: List the top 3 toughest questions asked by Wall Street analysts and assess whether management answered directly. 4. Key Surprises: Highlight any unexpected surprises vs consensus estimates.
Prompt 2: Peer Group Comparable Multiples Table Generation
Act as a financial modeling associate. Build a peer comparison valuation table for [Target Company] against its top 4 competitors: [Peer A, Peer B, Peer C, Peer D]. Format the output as a Markdown table with: - Ticker & Company Name - Current Market Capitalization ($/₹) - Trailing & Forward P/E Multiple - EV/EBITDA Multiple - 3-Year Historical Revenue CAGR - Return on Equity (ROE) Conclude with median multiples and identify whether the target trades at a discount or premium.

8. Financial Terms Glossary

Alpha (α)

The excess return of an investment relative to the return of a benchmark index.

Beta (β)

A statistical measure of a stock's volatility in relation to the overall market.

WACC

Weighted Average Cost of Capital — the blended hurdle rate required to discount future cash flows.

Enterprise Value (EV)

Market Capitalization + Total Debt - Cash & Cash Equivalents.
